# GLITTERS Construction Optimization Engine (`glitters_construction_engine` v2.0.0)

Phase 1 Production-Hardened Release addressing all Independent Technical Audit findings:
1. **Mandatory Material Strength & Verifiable Professional Engineering Authority (`CRITICAL 1`)**: `EngineeringSpecification.validate_compliance()` strictly rejects `None` concrete or steel strengths whenever minimums are specified, and requires a cryptographically verifiable `ProfessionalEngineeringApproval` token from the GLITTERS CORE registry (rejecting synthetic/fake registration IDs and expired licenses in production).
2. **Signed Measurement Evidence & Input vs. Derived Provenance Separation (`CRITICAL 2`)**: Removed self-declared `is_verified_measurement: bool`. Quantity takeoff requires a `SignedMeasurementEvidence` record (with BORAQS/ISK registration, date, SHA-256 document hash, and CORE signature) and separates `input_geometry_status` (`VERIFIED_MEASUREMENT`) from `derived_material_status` (`CALCULATED_ESTIMATE`).
3. **Production Quotation Gating (`CRITICAL 3`)**: Introduced `QuotationVerificationStatus` (`VERIFIED_BINDING_QUOTATION`, `SYNTHETIC_BENCHMARK_QUOTATION`, `EXPIRED_QUOTATION`, `UNVERIFIED_DRAFT_QUOTATION`). Production procurement rejects synthetic, unverified, or expired quotes.
4. **Full BOQ Completeness & 6-Metric Cost Evidence Coverage (`CRITICAL 4`)**: Validates mandatory sections, strictly positive quantities, and complete pricing, and computes separate coverage ratios for verified measurements, quotations, labour rates, equipment rates, and overall cost evidence.
5. **Non-Renewable Material Inventory Coupling, 2-Stage Lexicographic RCPSP & SSGS Validation (`CRITICAL 5`, `HIGH 6`, `HIGH 7`)**: Enforces non-renewable material caps and cumulative delivery schedules in RCPSP, optimizes makespan then direct cost in two exact stages, raises `InfeasibleScheduleError` on SSGS horizon exhaustion, runs an independent post-schedule constraint validator, and provides a built-in SciPy MILP fallback when Google OR-Tools is not installed.
